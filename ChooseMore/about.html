About ChooseMore

ChooseMore is a storefront demonstration built around practical browsing: explore sample items, compare illustrative details, and preview a simple shopping and product-help flow.

Demo scope: Product names, images, prices, and specifications are original samples. Checkout does not place an order or process payment. The local enquiry API only validates a preview request; it does not create support tickets, verify orders, or retain customer details.

ChooseMore support at a glance
AreaDetails
Sample catalog Browse the collection by category, inspect illustrative product specifications, and add sample items to a temporary bag.
Product guidance Have the product name, model or part number, serial number when relevant, and a clear description of the issue ready. Check the latest manufacturer instructions before use.
Order enquiries Prepare an enquiry preview for a sample product issue, replacement, or service request. It is not sent to a support team or matched to a live order.
Service and warranty Repair, installation, return, and warranty terms depend on the provider and product. Confirm eligibility and service availability directly with the original seller or manufacturer.
Safety Do not rely on general web guidance for electrical, industrial, medical, or other safety-critical work. Follow product instructions and consult a qualified professional.
Contact and accounts Account access and live support channels are not connected. Use fictional details in any preview form; form data is not retained.
