ChooseMore Support Center

Start with the type of issue you have. This page helps you prepare for a conversation with the seller, manufacturer, or service provider responsible for your product or order.

Get ready to ask for help

Product setup & compatibility

  • Have the product name, model or part number, and serial number ready.
  • Describe what you are connecting it to and what you expected to happen.
  • Check the latest manufacturer instructions before changing settings or parts.

Useful details

Model, operating system or equipment, accessories used, and the steps already tried.

Orders & delivery

  • Contact the seller for order status, delivery changes, cancellation, or a missing parcel.
  • Keep your order number and the email or phone used at checkout available.
  • Use the seller’s official website or app to protect your account.

Useful details

Order number, purchase date, delivery address region, and a short description of the issue.

Payments & refunds

  • Ask the seller about a charge, refund status, invoice, or payment method.
  • Check the transaction in your bank or payment provider’s official app.
  • Never share a PIN, password, one-time code, or full card number.

Useful details

Order number and transaction date. Do not send sensitive payment credentials.

Returns, warranty & repairs

  • Review the seller’s return window and the manufacturer’s warranty terms.
  • Ask an authorized service provider about repair options and coverage.
  • Keep proof of purchase and photos of visible damage if it is safe to do so.

Useful details

Purchase date, product model, proof of purchase, and a clear description of the fault.

Services & account access

  • For installation, maintenance, or bookings, contact the provider named on your service documents.
  • For sign-in problems, use the account provider’s official recovery process.
  • ChooseMore account and live support features are not connected in this preview.

Useful details

Service reference or account email address. Never share your password or one-time code.

Important: ChooseMore does not have a monitored phone line, email inbox, live chat, order lookup, or support ticket system connected. Contact the responsible company through a verified official channel.

Before you contact a provider

  1. Identify whether the issue is about a product, order, payment, return, warranty, repair, or service booking.
  2. Gather the order number or product model, purchase date, and the account email used with the provider.
  3. Explain what happened, when it started, and any troubleshooting steps already tried.
  4. Use the provider’s official website, app, or paperwork to confirm its contact details.

Do not share passwords, one-time codes, full payment-card details, or sensitive medical information. For safety-critical products, follow the manufacturer’s instructions and consult a qualified professional.

ChooseMore contact channels

Phone, email, and live chat: Not connected yet.

The storefront and enquiry screens are demonstrations only; ChooseMore does not operate a live support desk or order system. For an active purchase, warranty, repair, or service booking, contact the original seller, manufacturer, or provider through its official channel.
